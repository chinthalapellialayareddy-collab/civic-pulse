About CivicPulse

Bridging the trust and communication gap between citizens and municipal engineering divisions through artificial intelligence, algorithmic transparency, and geospatial precision.

THE PROBLEM

Why CivicPulse was Created

Traditional civic grievance redressal channels suffer from deep systemic hurdles: opaque progress tracking, duplicate complaint cluttering, lack of clear photographic proof of resolution, and cumbersome reporting interfaces.

CivicPulse transforms municipal accountability into a transparent, cooperative ecosystem where every citizen report is treated as a priority data point.

Core Engineering Pillars

Gemini AI Triage

Automatic categorization, intelligent priority determination, and concise issue summaries with zero manual clerical lag.

Duplicate Cluster Detection

Geographic radius matching (Haversine formula) prevents redundant work orders and consolidates public upvotes.

Before-and-After Verification

No complaint can be resolved without an official repair photograph and verified field engineer remarks.

Bilingual Inclusion
