MUNICIPAL DOMAINS

Core Problem Categories

CivicPulse routes each issue to specialized municipal engineering departments with intelligent AI prioritization.

Road Damage

Potholes, broken medians, asphalt erosion, dangerous craters and cave-ins.

Garbage Overflow

Uncollected street garbage, overflowing municipal dumpsters, litter accumulation.

Water Leakage

Clean pipeline bursts, municipal valve leaks, water supply pressure drop.

Streetlight Failure

Non-working junction lights, broken electrical poles, dark vulnerable stretches.

Drainage Problems

Open hazardous manholes, sewage blockages, rainwater stagnation on roads.

Public Infrastructure

Broken bus stops, footpaths, fallen branches, damaged public park amenities.

How CivicPulse Works

3
