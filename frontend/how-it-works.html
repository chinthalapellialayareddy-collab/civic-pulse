STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

How CivicPulse Operates

A streamlined pipeline engineered to ensure accountability, prevent duplicate tasks, and deliver verifiable results.

1

Issue Reporting with Geolocation & Photos

Citizens snap a photo of the road damage, water leak, or overflowing garbage. The platform uses the Browser Geolocation API or Nominatim geocoding to automatically pinpoint exact latitude and longitude on OpenStreetMap.

2

AI Classification & Prioritization

Powered by Google Gemini (with resilient rule-based fallbacks), the system suggests the appropriate category, assigns an urgency priority score (Low to Critical), and produces an executive summary for triage officials.

3

Duplicate Cluster Detection & Community Support

Before logging, the system scans active complaints within a 600m radius using the Haversine formula. If neighbors already reported it, citizens can "Upvote/Support" the issue, boosting urgency rather than cluttering municipal dispatch.

4

Department Routing & Dispatch

Municipal controllers assign the ticket to specialized teams: Roads & Bridges, Solid Waste Management, or Water Works. Real-time updates notify citizens in-app and via Resend email.

5

Before-and-After Verified Resolution

Once ground crews fix the problem, administrators upload the official resolution photograph. The original condition and completed repair are shown side-by-side on the public tracker for 100% civic accountability.
